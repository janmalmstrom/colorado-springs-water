What We Do

Water Damage Restoration Services — Colorado Springs

Every water damage situation is different. Burst pipe, basement flood, sewage backup, appliance failure — each requires a different protocol. Here is what we handle.

Water Extraction

Commercial truck-mounted extraction units remove thousands of gallons per hour — far more than consumer vacuums or mop-and-bucket. We extract standing water and water trapped in carpet, pad, and drywall before drying begins.

Structural Drying

Industrial dehumidifiers and high-velocity air movers placed strategically around the structure. Daily moisture readings document drying progress. We don't stop until moisture levels return to acceptable levels per IICRC S500 standards.

Flood Damage Cleanup

Basement flooding from snowmelt, heavy rain, or storm drainage. Colorado Springs spring runoff and severe afternoon thunderstorms are common causes. We handle standing water removal, mud cleanup, and structural assessment.

Sewage & Contaminated Water

Category 3 black water (sewage, floodwater from outside) cannot be dried — contaminated materials must be removed. We follow IICRC Category 3 protocol: containment, removal, antimicrobial treatment, and air scrubbing.

Mold Remediation

Water damage that wasn't dried properly — or was unknown for days or weeks — often leads to mold. We assess, contain, and remediate mold growth per IICRC S520 standard. Clearance testing coordination available.

Burst Pipe & Appliance Failure

Colorado's freeze-thaw cycles cause pipe failures every winter. Dishwashers, washing machines, water heaters, and refrigerator lines fail year-round. We respond same-day to prevent damage from spreading.

Commercial Water Damage

Office buildings, retail spaces, and multi-unit properties. We understand the urgency of getting a business operational. Commercial jobs are staffed appropriately to minimize downtime.

Insurance Claim Assistance

We provide complete moisture documentation, photo records, and itemized scope of work in the format your insurance adjuster needs. We work directly with all major carriers and can communicate with your adjuster on your behalf.

Why Speed Matters

Water Damage Gets Worse Every Hour

This is not a scare tactic — it is the documented timeline used in the IICRC S500 standard. The faster water is extracted and drying begins, the lower the total damage and the lower the final claim.

1h

First Hour: Spread & Absorption

Water migrates under flooring, into wall cavities, and through drywall. Carpet pad absorbs and holds water. The longer it sits, the more surface area is affected and the more material must be replaced.

24h

24 Hours: Drywall Damage Begins

Drywall swells, warps, and begins to lose structural integrity. Hardwood flooring starts to cup. Subfloor materials begin absorbing water. Furniture may be stained beyond recovery.

48h

48 Hours: Mold Growth Can Begin

The IICRC S520 standard identifies 24–48 hours as the window in which mold can begin growing on wet organic materials at normal room temperatures. Once mold is present, the scope and cost of remediation increases significantly.

7d

One Week: Structural Compromise

Framing lumber, subfloor OSB, and structural components begin to deteriorate with prolonged saturation. What started as a $5,000 extraction-and-drying job can become a $25,000+ structural rebuild.
